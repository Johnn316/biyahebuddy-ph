# Sources

Every law, fine, hotline and coding rule shipped in `assets/data/` must have an
official source listed here before demo day. Entries in the JSON prefixed with
`SAMPLE` are placeholders and are **not verified**.

Owner: Devin (data). Reviewers: John, backend teammate.

## Laws (`laws.json`)

Generated by `tools/build_data.py` from the raw files in `data_sources/raw/`
(see "How to rebuild data" in the README). Every chunk in `laws.json` carries
its `url` and `as_of` (retrieval date). Download status per source is recorded
in `data_sources/raw/manifest.json`.

Blocked sites (2026-10-09): `lto.gov.ph` and `officialgazette.gov.ph` both
return HTTP 403 with a Cloudflare "verify you are human" challenge to scripts
and to the automated browser, so the RAs were taken from lawphil.net (Arellano
Law Foundation) and the LTO Driver's Manual is not included yet. To add it,
download the PDF manually from lto.gov.ph, save it as
`data_sources/raw/lto_drivers_manual.pdf`, add a `lto-manual` entry
(`url`, `retrieved`) to `manifest.json`, and rerun the build.

### Amendments noted in `laws.json`

Lawphil publishes the original text of each act. Where one of the acts above
explicitly amends or repeals a section of another, the build adds a `notes`
entry to the affected chunks:

| Affected section | Amended / repealed by |
|---|---|
| RA 4136, Sec. 34 (accessories) | RA 8750, Sec. 15 — "hereby amended" (seat belts) |
| RA 4136, Sec. 56(f) (drunk-driving penalty) | RA 10586, Sec. 19 — repealed |
| RA 8750, Sec. 5 (children in front seat) | RA 11229, Sec. 17 — "hereby amended" |
| RA 8750, Sec. 11 | RA 11229, Sec. 17 — "hereby amended" |

Amendments made by laws outside this list (e.g. later amendments to RA 4136's
license provisions) are **not** reflected.
